# App Details
**Name**: DailyHabit
**Short Description**: Build habits, track your progress, and stay motivated every day.
**Full Description**: DailyHabit is a simple and colorful app for building and sticking to good habits, one day at a time. Choose from predefined habits or create your own, assign a color to each one for quick visual identification, and swipe to mark them as done as you move through your day.
With DailyHabit you can personalize your profile, check your weekly progress in the Reports section, and set up notifications for the habits that matter most to you, choosing the time of day you'd like to be reminded.
Perfect for anyone looking for a clear and visual way to build healthy routines, without the hassle.
